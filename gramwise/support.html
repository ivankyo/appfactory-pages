GramWise — Support

Cups to grams for every ingredient, plus oven and pan conversions

Where do the ingredient weights come from?

Each ingredient has a density measured in grams per cup, taken from standard baking references and rounded the way recipes are written.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole ingredient database ships inside the app.

Can I add my own ingredient?

Yes. Add a custom ingredient with its own weight per cup and it appears alongside the built-in ones.
