PaceLab — Support

Pace, splits and race predictions for runners

How are race predictions calculated?

PaceLab uses the Riegel formula, which scales a known result to another distance with an endurance exponent. It is an estimate, not a promise.

Does the app track my runs?

No. PaceLab does not use GPS, motion data or any sensors — it only does arithmetic on numbers you type.

Is it medical or training advice?

No. The heart-rate zones are the standard percentage bands and are offered for reference only.
