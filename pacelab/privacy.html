Privacy Policy

PaceLab · last updated 2026-08-19

PaceLab does not collect any personal data. Everything the app stores stays on the device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no third-party trackers, and nothing is uploaded to a server.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and any content you create in the app are stored locally using the operating system's standard storage. Deleting the app removes them.

Third parties

The app does not share data with third parties. If you contact support by email, that email is used only to answer your question.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
