TimeZip — Support

See every time zone at once and find the hour that works for everyone

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The full time zone database ships inside the app, so everything works offline.

How are daylight saving changes handled?

TimeZip uses the IANA time zone database, so DST transitions are applied automatically for every city.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your cities and settings never leave your device.
